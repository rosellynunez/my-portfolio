Festina and Lotus Connected D Landing Pages Design

Website Design & Development for "Introduction to Automated Negotiation"

Project Overview

Festina and Lotus, two distinct brands under the Festina Group, each launched a new Connected Watch Collection:

  • Festina Connected D: A classic, elegant smartwatch blending tradition with technology.
  • Lotus Hybrid Connected: A youthful, modern smartwatch designed for an active lifestyle.

the challenge

The challenge was to create two distinct landing pages that captured each brand's unique identity, showcased the watch features, and provided a cohesive user experience. Using a shared page structure was key to managing the tight deadlines and ensuring a consistent look and feel for both projects.

my role

My role focused on designing the UI for both landing pages, creating a consistent visual journey for the user. This involved close collaboration with different teams to bring the projects to life successfully

  • Development Team - To ensure smooth implementation using Strapi and Bootstrap
  • Marketing Team (Spain)- To align design with local branding and promotional campaigns
  • Strategy Team (Sweden)- To define the user journey and messaging.

1. Design strategy

Concept for Festina Connected D

The Festina landing page needed to reflect the brand's core values: Quality, Tradition, and Technology. The goal was to create a sophisticated interface that balanced:

  • Smart functionality - Highlighting watch features with a clean and structured layout
  • Elegance - Using and elegant typography, refined spacing, and a balanced color palette
  • Consistency - Ensuring that the Lotus Connected D also fit within the same user journey

Concept for Lotus Hybrid Connected

For Lotus, the focus was on versatility and modernity. The design emphasized:

  • Dynamic lifestyle appeal - Showcasing how the watch fits into everyday life
  • Engaging visuals - Featuring bold, high-contrast images to highlight key features like smart notifications and activity tracking.
  • Clean and modern UI - Using large, easy-to-read text and elements for smooth navigation

2. Optimizing images

I received the graphic assets from the design team, and they were, generously sized. Way too big for the web. I adjusted image dimensions to align with the design specifications, preventing unnecessary bandwidth consumption.

Of course, it had to look good everywhere, so I optimized for both desktop and mobile. And to really boost speed, converted everything to WebP.

3. UI/UX Approach

To ensure an engaging and seamless experience, I focused on visual appeal and functionality, guided by these principles:

  • Navigation & User Flow - Simple and intuitive layout guiding users through product features
  • Visual Hierarchy - Strong CTAs like "See Collection" to encourage user engagement
  • White Space & Balance - Creating a clean and distraction-free browsing experience
  • Component-Based Design - Using our existing components to streamline development

4. And just one new component

One addition was a new component designed specifically for, large, eye-catching images to grab attention and highlight key products. Figma plugins and Storybook streamlined the design-to-development process.

New component: fgp-ui-text-on-background

  • Purpose: Text overlay component for impactful hero images and background sections
  • Application: Used to highlight key features dynamically.
  • Flexibility: Customizable text position, alignment, and size for a responsive design across devices.

You might notice the component names are a bit of a head-scratcher. We're keeping them consistent for now, since everything's interconnected, but a naming overhaul is definitely on our to-do list.

5. Post-development QA & refinements

Following development, my process included comprehensive quality assurance to guarantee that the final product maintained design consistency with the original Figma prototypes, delivered optimized image performance on desktop and mobile, and provided a seamless, interactive user experience on every device.

6. Final results

After development, I conducted thorough QA testing, ensuring:

  • 🚀 Successfully launched two visually compelling landing pages.
  • 🎯 Each page captured the essence of its respective brand.
  • 🤝 Collaboration between UI, development, and marketing delivered a seamless user experience.

7. Key takeaways & learnings

First and foremost, this project underscored that clear communication is the foundation of effective collaboration. Whether I was talking with developers or stakeholders, explaining the "why" behind a design was just as important as the "what."

Secondly, I got to dive into the classic tug-of-war between SEO and UX. I advocated for a clean, engaging interface, believing that a great user experience is what ultimately keeps people on a site. Though we ended up with more text than I'd initially designed to satisfy SEO requirements, the process taught me how to build a strong, user-focused case and the art of strategic compromise in a cross-functional team.
