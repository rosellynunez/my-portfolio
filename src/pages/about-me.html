A little about me

The human behind the pixels

I design systems that make complexity feel simple.

As a UX/UI and Design Systems Designer, I focus on bringing structure to complexity and turning ideas into clear, usable experiences.

I love collaborating with cross-functional teams — co-creation is truly my jam 😋 — and I'm always chasing new perspectives that challenge me to grow as a designer and as a human.

Previously, I worked for 5+ years in graphic design before transitioning into UX/UI and Design Systems. Different titles, same thing: making things clearer, better, and more human.

Good design is not decoration. It is clarity, structure, and intention.

From sketchbook to digital world

Born and raised in Peru 🇵🇪, where my love for drawing naturally led me to graphic design.

After working for some years as a graphic designer, I moved to Barcelona for a Master's in UX/UI Design at Elisava School .

Now, let's just say I'm pretty happy to call Barcelona my home 🏡

When I am not in front of my screen...

One of my favorite activities is co-hosting an improvisation meetup .

It’s a great way to connect with others and laugh a lot. So if you're in Barcelona, you're very welcome to come.

Besides that, I love exploring the world — trying new foods, discovering different cultures, and collecting fresh perspectives.

Recently, I’ve been fascinated by Japan’s balance between tradition and technology, and by the spiritual life in India. I’ve also picked up drawing again.
