From sketchbook to digital world

Born and raised in Peru 🇵🇪, where my love for drawing as a kid naturally led me to graphic design. After working for years as a graphic designer, I packed my bags and headed to Barcelona for a Master's in UX/UI Design at Elisava School.

Now, let's just say I'm pretty happy to call Barcelona my home 🏡.

When I am not in front of my screen...

One of my favorite activities is co-hosting an improvisation meetup. It's a great way to connect with others and laugh a lot! So, if you are in Barcelona, you're welcome to come.

Beside that, I'm a big fan of exploring the world. Trying new foods, experiencing different cultures, and seeing amazing scenery inspires me and gives me fresh perspectives.

Recently, I've been fascinated by Japan's mixture of tradition and technology, and the spiritual life in India. Also, I've picked up drawing again.
