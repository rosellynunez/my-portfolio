From sketchbook
to digital world

Born and raised in Peru 🇵🇪, where my love for drawing as a kid naturally led me to graphic design. After working for years as a graphic designer, I packed my bags and headed to Barcelona for a Master's in UX/UI Design at Elisava School.

Now, let's just say I'm pretty happy to call Barcelona my home 🏡.
